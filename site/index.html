01The problem

On-chain lending is poker with your cards face up.

On Aave or Morpho, anyone can look up your collateral, your debt and the exact price at which you get liquidated, and tie all of it to your address. Bots wait for that price. If the position is big enough, traders try to push the market there.

FundsCompetitors watch the treasury and every move in and out.
FoundersBorrowing against your own token looks like dumping it.
TradersA public liquidation price is a target to push the price to.
EveryoneA big balance tied to your name invites theft and extortion.
In 2023, Curve's founder had over $100M borrowed against his own token. Everyone could see his liquidation price, traders shorted toward it, and he sold tokens about a third below market to get out. Half of that story is that everyone knew the loan was his.
02What Bosphorus changes

The market still sees the position. Nobody sees whose it is.

Positions stay public on purpose: anyone can check health and liquidate, so lenders are protected the way they are on any market.

Dive

Move tokens from your everyday account into a private account. People see money leave, not where it went.

Act privately

Add collateral, borrow, repay, withdraw. Each op is a private transaction proven on your laptop: your keys and your position's secret never leave it.

Settle in public

The sequencer verifies the proof and the market updates. Each position gets an unrelated address, and no public account signs or pays.

03Features

A real money market, with privacy built in.

◐

Private owners

Positions are held by private accounts. Nothing published links one back to its owner, or two positions to each other.

▦

Isolated markets

Morpho Blue-style markets: immutable, one collateral and one loan asset each, with an Aave-style interest curve.

⚡

GPU prover

Our own Metal kernels and proving schedule turn a 10-minute private borrow into about 47 seconds on a laptop.

⛨

Screened pool

Privacy Pools on LEZ: a screener approves clean deposits without learning who withdraws. Unapproved? Take your deposit back.

✓

Solvency proofs

Prove "one of these positions is mine and it's healthy" to an auditor in 6–10 seconds, without saying which one.

⌂

Runs on your laptop

App, client, maths, prover and wallet all run locally. No server, no account with us. Ships as a Basecamp module.

↯

Open liquidations

An open-source keeper finds unhealthy positions by scanning blocks and liquidates them, private owners included.

⇋

Lenders stay private too

Hold supply shares inside your own private account: no public position at all, only the market totals move.

◇

Built for LEZ

Privacy is an account type on Logos, so the lending program runs inside your own proof. No custom shielded pool or verifier.

04The optimized prover

Ten minutes per click isn't a product. So we fixed the prover.

The proving library LEZ uses had no Apple GPU support. We wrote Metal kernels for it, bit-exact with the CPU code, and a schedule that executes everything first and then proves on two GPU lanes.

05Honest about it

What the water doesn't hide yet.

  • Position contents are public. Collateral, debt and liquidation price stay visible so anyone can liquidate. Hiding them is the next design.
  • Amounts and timing can link steps. Use unrelated amounts, and let time pass between a dive and a deposit.
  • The anonymity set is small while few people use private mode.
  • Experimental and not audited. Our own self-review and its findings are public in the repo.
